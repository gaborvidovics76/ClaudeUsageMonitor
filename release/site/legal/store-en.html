Privacy Policy – Usage Monitor for Claude AI (Microsoft Store version)

Effective from 2026-10-26. This policy applies to the version installed from the Microsoft Store. The Hungarian version is binding; this is a translation.

Appointing a data protection officer is not mandatory, so there is none.

2. In short

The program shows the usage of your own Claude subscription. No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, no profiling, and no data is ever sold. The Store version does not check for updates: the Microsoft Store delivers them.

3. What stays on your PC

  • It reads the local usage log of Claude Desktop (if installed) and the local logs of Claude Code: only the time, the model name and the token counts are used, never the content of any message.
  • Settings and up to 7 days of history are kept in the program's own folder on your PC.
  • The claude.ai sign-in token is stored encrypted (Windows DPAPI), on this PC only, readable only by your user account.

None of this leaves your PC, and the controller has no access to it.

4. Connection to Anthropic (only if you choose the “claude.ai” data source)

After you sign in, the program asks Anthropic's servers directly, with your own account, for your usage and the name of your plan (api.anthropic.com, platform.claude.com, claude.ai). This traffic is between you and Anthropic; it never reaches the controller. Anthropic is an independent controller for it, under its own privacy policy.
