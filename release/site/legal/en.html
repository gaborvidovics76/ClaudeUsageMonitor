3. Cookies and similar techniques

This page uses no cookies, contains no tracking code and loads nothing from third parties (even the font is served from here). Only your chosen language is kept in your browser's local storage – it never leaves your device.

The page works fully without cookies. Your chosen language and your cookie decision are kept in your browser's local storage.

Google measurement – only with your consent. If you allow it in the cookie notice, Google Analytics (and, if you separately allow it, Google Ads conversion measurement) sets cookies and sends visit data (shortened IP address, device and browser data, sections viewed, download clicks) to Google. Until you decide, or if you reject, no Google code is loaded at all (Consent Mode v2, default: denied). Legal basis: consent – GDPR Art. 6(1)(a). You can change your decision at any time under “Cookie settings” in the footer. Processor: Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland); transfers to the United States rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Measurement data is retained for up to 14 months.

reCAPTCHA – at the forms only. To keep robots from sending junk, the forms are protected by Google reCAPTCHA v3. Google's script is not loaded with the page, only when you click into one of the forms; Google (Google Ireland Limited) then processes device and browser data and your IP address and returns a score that says how likely you are to be a human. Legal basis: legitimate interest in preventing abuse – GDPR Art. 6(1)(f). Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
