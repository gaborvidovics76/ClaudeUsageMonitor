# Glossary – Bahasa Indonesia (`id`)

Claude Usage Monitor UI. One fixed translation per term; the module `claude_usage/langs/id.py` follows this list.

## Tone and form of address

- **Address: "Anda"** (always capitalised, as Microsoft, Google and Apple do in Indonesian UI). Never "kamu", never "Saudara".
- Register: friendly but proper – the Microsoft/Apple Bahasa Indonesia UI register. Short imperatives without "silakan"
  ("Masuk lagi.", "Periksa koneksi Anda"); "silakan"/"harap" only where the English says "please".
- Spelling: EYD V (2022). Prefix "antar-" is written together (antarmodel), "di-" passive on loanwords without hyphen
  ("direset"). No unnecessary English where a standard Indonesian term exists; established Microsoft loanwords are kept
  (file, folder, server, browser, mouse, log, email, hash, online, header, snapshot, endpoint, platform).
- The author (Vidovics Gábor) speaks in first person as "saya" in the feedback window; the project speaks as "kami"
  in the liability texts.
- Punctuation: en dash with spaces (" – ") as in the English (also where the English has a plain hyphen); "mis." for
  "e.g."; percent sign attached to the number per EYD ("70%"); thousands/decimals are not used.

## Privacy document name

**"Kebijakan Privasi"** – the term used by Google, Microsoft, Apple, Tokopedia, Gojek and Indonesian websites for the
privacy notice, and by the data-protection discourse in Indonesia. Capitalised as a document title. The GDPR stays
"GDPR" with its article numbers ("GDPR Pasal 6 ayat (1) huruf f"). Indonesian law (UU PDP) is NOT claimed; the
supervisory authority is "otoritas pengawas … atau otoritas di negara Anda".
