# Glossary – Greek (el)

Scope: the whole UI (358 keys of `source.json` + the 4 macOS overrides). Target quality: the Greek of Windows,
Office and macOS – the words the user already sees in the operating system.

## Tone and form of address

- **Polite plural "εσείς"** everywhere, as Microsoft and Apple do in Greek ("Κάντε κλικ…", "Ελέγξτε τη σύνδεσή σας",
  "Συνδεθείτε ξανά"). Greek consumer software never uses the singular "εσύ" in instructions; the plural is
  neutral, not stiff. Imperatives are in the plural ("Επικολλήστε", "Γράψτε", "Δοκιμάστε ξανά").
- The author speaks in the **first person** in the feedback window ("Πείτε μου", "Διαβάζω κάθε μήνυμα").
- Short, friendly, confident – no bureaucratic "παρακαλούμε όπως…". "Παρακαλώ" appears only where the English has
  "please".
- Final-ν rule of standard Modern Greek is applied (τον/την before vowels and κ, π, τ, ξ, ψ, γκ, μπ, ντ, τσ, τζ;
  "τη σύνδεση", "την πολιτική").

## UPPERCASE panel labels

Greek typography drops the tonos in all-capital words: **ΣΥΝΕΔΡΙΑ 5 ΩΡΩΝ**, **ΕΒΔΟΜΑΔΙΑΙΟ ΟΡΙΟ**, **ΕΒΔ.**,
**ΑΠΕΤΥΧΕ**, **ΕΠΙΣΗΜΟΣ ΙΣΤΟΤΟΠΟΣ**. Mixed-case text keeps its accents as normal. (The checker enforces this.)

## Sign in / sign out (Microsoft terminology)

- **Είσοδος** = Sign in, **Έξοδος** = Sign out – the Windows / Microsoft-account wording. (Google uses
  Σύνδεση/Αποσύνδεση; we follow Microsoft because the app lives in the Windows notification area.)
- In running sentences the verb is **συνδέομαι**: "Συνδεθείτε ξανά", "Δεν έχετε συνδεθεί" – this is also how
  Windows phrases its own instructions, and there is no natural verb for "είσοδος".
- Because Microsoft also uses **Έξοδος** for *Quit/Exit*, the sign-out item always names its object:
  "Έξοδος από το claude.ai"; plain "Έξοδος" is the app's Quit.

## Privacy document name

**Πολιτική απορρήτου** – the name used by Google, Apple, Meta and the large Greek sites, and the term Greek
users expect on a link and a checkbox. Microsoft's own "Δήλωση προστασίας προσωπικών δεδομένων" is longer and
less common outside Microsoft. The legal body of the notice (`fb.privacy_text`) uses the official Greek GDPR
vocabulary of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 = **ΓΚΠΔ** (Γενικός Κανονισμός για την Προστασία Δεδομένων).
