## English (United States)

**Description**

```
Claude stops at a limit – usually in the middle of something important. Usage Monitor for Claude AI shows you, all the time, how much of your Claude subscription is left and exactly when it comes back, so you can plan your work instead of being surprised.

WHAT YOU SEE AT A GLANCE
• The 5-hour session limit and the weekly limit, with a countdown to the next reset
• The weekly limit of a single model when your plan has one (for example Fable)
• Burn rate and pace: are you using the week faster than it passes?
• Which limit applies right now – highlighted
• A small trend line under every gauge

EVERYTHING ELSE YOUR ACCOUNT REPORTS
• Limits of other models, connected apps and routines
• Usage credits (pay-as-you-go) with the monthly limit
• Saved limit resets: how many you have and when the first one expires
• This week's split by product – Claude Code, chats, Cowork – across all your devices
• From Claude Code's own logs on this PC: how your week is split between the models

STAYS OUT OF YOUR WAY
• Always on top, or only in the tray; the tray icon itself shows the percentage
• Three layouts (card, slim bar, rings), six themes, your own accent colour, four sizes
• Click-through mode, lock in place, snap to screen edges, choose the order of the gauges
• Quiet notifications at the thresholds you set, when a limit resets, and before a saved reset expires
• History window: 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days, with peak, daily average and an end-of-week projection
• 34 languages

TWO WAYS TO GET THE NUMBERS
• Local: reads the usage log Claude Desktop keeps on this PC – no sign-in at all.
• claude.ai: sign in once with your own Claude account (in your browser, the way Claude Code does it) and see the server's figures for all your devices – phone, browser and other computers included.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, no account with us. The program talks only to Anthropic's servers, with your own sign-in, and – only if you send one – the optional "Message to the developer" form. The sign-in is stored encrypted on this PC. The source code is public (MIT licence), so anyone can check this.

WHAT YOU NEED
A Claude subscription (Pro, Max or Team), and either Claude Desktop on this PC or a claude.ai sign-in.

Free. Unofficial community tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. "Claude" is a trademark of Anthropic, PBC.
```

## Why it is written this way (persuasion, kept honest)

| Technique | Where | Honesty check |
|---|---|---|
| Focus on a recognisable moment (T08 similarity, T07 focus) | first sentence: "stops at a limit – in the middle of something important" | describes what the user already knows happens; no exaggeration |
| Gain framing (T10) | "plan your work instead of being surprised" | the product really shows reset times and pace |
| Concrete, checkable facts (T01 availability) | 6 themes, 3 layouts, 34 languages, 6 h / 24 h / 7 days | each counted in the program |
| Risk reduction | "free", "no account with us", "source code is public", "what you need" | true; the requirements section stops people who could not use it |
| Peak–end (T30) | ends on free / unofficial / trademark | the last line is the honest one |

Not used on purpose: no user counts, no ratings, no "best", no "official", no time pressure – none of these could be proved.
